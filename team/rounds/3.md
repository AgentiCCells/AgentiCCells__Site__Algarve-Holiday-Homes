## Architecture (conductor's decisions)

1. **One state, in the browser.** `localStorage["cc.sim.v1"]`: a JSON document with the clock, homes' rates, bookings, guests (invented or typed by the visitor, kept only in this browser), payments (pretend), stock, issues, reviews, messages, a ledger, and the policy log. Every change is an event appended to a log, so the admin, the world and the agents replay the same history. Tabs and iframes stay in step through the `storage` event and a BroadcastChannel. A "reset the simulation" button on every mode page. Nothing leaves the browser; the validator's no-POST rule stays.
2. **The clock and the real-data window.** The simulated "today" moves on a slider, bounded by the dates for which real data exists (team/rounds/2/research/REAL-DATES.md): weather as IPMA issued it, from the live files (today plus four days; sea today plus two) and the vault copies of 17 November; events from 18 November to 31 December 2026. A day outside the weather window says plainly "no real forecast for this day yet"; nothing is invented.
3. **Modes and addresses.** Visitor: the main site (`/`, `/homes/<id>/`), now booking for real in the simulation; after the pretend payment, `/stay/` (the guest's trip: a day-by-day timeline, the weather and events for each day, a plan the guest builds, getting there from Faro airport, the agent). Admin: `/admin/` (today, bookings, calendar and occupancy, rates, stock, cleaning, issues, reviews, messages, money (P&L), legality, integrations, policies). Side by side: `/play/` (the visitor site and the admin in two iframes, one time slider, a log between them). World: `/world/` (round 4). Agents on both sides: `/agents/` (round 4). A mode switcher in the header and an "About this demo" panel: "This is a demo of ..." listing what is real (IPMA data, events), simulated (availability, bookings, money, people), invented (the company, homes, prices) and AI-generated (the pictures).
4. **Agent Behaviour Policies in the browser.** `src/assets/js/sim/abp.js`: each actor (the site's agent, the guest's assistant, the host's assistant, each synthetic user) has a policy in the same agenticcells-abp/0.1 format as `team/`, and every action goes through one guard that answers allow, ask a person, or deny, with the rule's id, and logs it. Text from untrusted places (reviews, messages, guest notes, event titles) is marked as untrusted; any action whose instruction traces back to untrusted text is denied (the prompt-injection demonstration). The log is visible on every mode page.
5. **Money and law.** The P&L uses the formulas in team/rounds/2/research/LEGAL-PT.md (VAT 6 percent, the municipal tourist tax where one exists, IRS simplified regime), every figure marked as a simulation and "not legal or tax advice".
6. **Booking platforms** are named only by type of service (Dinis, about 17:45 UTC), for example "holiday-rental booking platforms" and "calendar feeds (iCal)".
