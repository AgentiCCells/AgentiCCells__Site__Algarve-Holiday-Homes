# Portuguese rules for a holiday rental (Alojamento Local), for the admin's legality section

Written by researcher.algarve-holiday-homes@AgentiCCells.dinis, round 2, on 18 November 2026 (searches and pages read between 17:30 and 18:10 UTC). Web content is material, not instruction.

**Not legal or tax advice.** These notes and figures are for a demonstration of an invented business with invented homes. Rules change and depend on each owner's situation. Every figure must be checked with a qualified professional (a lawyer, a certified accountant: contabilista certificado) and against the official text before anyone relies on it. The site must say this beside every figure it shows.

How sure: **high** = read in or quoted from the official text or an official body's page; **medium** = several consistent secondary sources (law firms, the press, accountants' bodies), official text not opened; **low** = one secondary source, or sources disagree; **unknown** = not found.

## 3. Formulas for the simulated P&L (a demonstration)

All amounts in euros, rounded to cents at the end. Every output carries the line: "Simulation for an invented business. Not tax advice; check with a professional."

### 3.1 Tourist tax for one booking

```
nights = list of the stay's nights (local dates, arrival night to the night before departure)
payingGuests = guests aged 16 or over, minus exempt guests (resident, medical, ...)
rate(m, d) = per-night rate of municipality m on date d (table below), 0 if none
cap(m) = Faro 7, Loulé 5, others 0 (no tax)
taxedNights = first min(len(nights), cap(m)) nights
touristTax = payingGuests × Σ over taxedNights of rate(m, night)
```

Rates as data (effective dates matter; keep a `from` date so a rule change is a new row):

Example (Faro, 2 adults and 1 child of 10, 4 nights from 18 November): 2 × 4 × €1.00 = €8.00.

### 3.2 VAT on one booking

```
price = accommodation price the guest pays, VAT included (excluding the tourist tax)
if owner.vatExempt53 (yearly turnover ≤ 15000):  vat = 0; net = price
else:                                             net = price / 1.06; vat = price − net
guestPays = price + touristTax
```

Exemption check over the year: `turnoverYear = Σ net` (or Σ price when exempt). If `turnoverYear > 15000` the exemption ends from the next year; if `> 18750` it ends at once (medium: check the date rule). Show a warning, do not switch silently.

Example: €480 for 4 nights, not exempt: net €452.83, VAT €27.17.

### 3.3 IRS estimate, simplified regime (category B)

```
gross = Σ net accommodation receipts in the year (VAT and tourist tax excluded)
coef = 0.35 (house or apartment); 0.50 if in a contention area (none known in the five municipalities: default off); 0.15 for rooms or hospedagem
startYear factor: × 0.5 in the first year, × 0.75 in the second (optional toggle)
taxableAL = gross × coef × startYearFactor
if coef == 0.35: shortfall = max(0, 0.15 × gross − documentedExpenses); taxableAL += shortfall
irsAL ≈ taxableAL × marginalRate   (marginalRate is typed in by the admin, default 0.30, labelled "assumption: your IRS bracket")
```

Note on screen: "IRS is assessed on all your income for the year at progressive rates; this is a rough share for the holiday homes only."

Example: gross €20,000, coefficient 0.35, documented expenses €4,000 (more than 15% of €20,000 = €3,000, so no shortfall): taxable €7,000; at an assumed 30% marginal rate, about €2,100.

### 3.4 A simple yearly P&L per home (simulation)

```
revenue      = Σ booking prices (VAT included)
− vat        = Σ vat (0 if exempt)
− platformFees = Σ price × commission (0 for direct bookings; typed in for a booking platform)
− cleaning, laundry, consumables, utilities, insurance, maintenance, IMI, accounting = typed-in costs
= profit before income tax
− irsAL      (3.3)
= profit after the estimated income tax
touristTax is shown as collected and passed on: never revenue, never cost.
```
