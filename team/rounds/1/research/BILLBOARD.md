## What a digital billboard in London usually needs (public sources)

| Format | Size and resolution | Files | Slot | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Digital 6-sheet (portrait; bus shelters, malls, supermarkets, phone boxes) | 1080 x 1920 px | Static: RGB JPG, 150 dpi. Motion: MPEG4 or MOV, H.264, 25 fps, "10mg or less" (probably 10 MB). A static backup JPG is also required. | 10 seconds for motion copy | Bauer Media Outdoor, "6 sheet poster products", https://www.bauermediaoutdoor.co.uk/latest/6-sheet-poster-products (fetched 2026-11-18) |
| D48 (large roadside, landscape, 4:3) | about 240 x 180 cm; 2880 x 2160 px minimum on most networks | JPEG or PNG, RGB, 5 to 10 MB; most D48 screens static only, some take MP4 H.264 | 6 to 10 seconds per advert in rotation | media.co.uk blog, "London D48s: digital screen size and format specs", https://media.co.uk/blogs/blog/london-d48s-digital-screen-size-digital-format-specs (fetched 2026-11-18; the page says figures vary by network) |
| Generic landscape screens (16:9) | 1920 x 1080 px is the usual landscape size, 1080 x 1920 the usual portrait | JPG, PNG or MP4 (H.264) | varies | AdQuick, "Digital out-of-home (DOOH) creative specs and lead times", https://www.adquick.com/answers/digital-out-of-home-dooh-creative-specs-and-lead-times (seen in search results only, not read in full) |

Other points from the same sources and search results:

- Each media owner has its own sheet (Bauer/Adshel, Clear Channel, JCDecaux, Global); confirm with the chosen owner before making artwork. One owner asks for final files five working days before display (an oOh!media sheet, Australia, seen in search results; for illustration only).
- Live or data-driven copy ("dynamic DOOH") is generally done by the media owner's or an ad-server's system swapping pre-approved templates when data changes; a live video feed is a special arrangement. Not checked in depth: mark as to be confirmed.
- clearchannel.co.uk could not be fetched from this session (host not allowed), so Clear Channel figures are not recorded.
