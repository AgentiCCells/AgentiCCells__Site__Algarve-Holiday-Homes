Written by researcher.algarve-holiday-homes@AgentiCCells.dinis, 2026-11-18. Everything on this page is invented for a demonstration site, except the search results, which are recorded as found.

## Recommendation

**Cinco Chaminés** ("Five Chimneys"), shown on the site as "Cinco Chaminés, holiday homes in the Algarve (a made-up example)".

Why: short, easy to say in English and Portuguese, tied to a real Algarve building tradition (the decorated Algarve chimney), and one chimney per house gives the site a drawing motif. No searches found a holiday-rental, property or hospitality business using the name, and neither cincochamines.pt nor cincochamines.com exists in DNS. Runner-up if a person prefers: **Pedra Morna** ("warm stone"), which returned nothing at all.

Descriptions (invented):

- **The Salt House, Tavira.** A low white house with a roof terrace looking towards the salt pans and the river. A short walk to the old town; the island beach is a ferry ride away. Features: roof terrace, two bathrooms, washing machine, bicycles for two, air conditioning, fast internet.
- **The Lagoon House, Faro.** A small first-floor flat for two at the edge of the old town, near the lagoon and the boats to the islands. Good for a short break: the airport is a short drive. Features: balcony, kitchenette, air conditioning, fast internet, step-free entrance from the street door to the lift.
- **The Hill House, near Loulé.** A country house among carob and almond trees in the hills behind Loulé, ten minutes by car from the town market. Space for a family or two couples. Features: private pool, garden with shade, barbecue, three bathrooms, fireplace for winter, parking. A car is needed.
- **The Harbour House, Lagos.** A two-bedroom town house a few streets from the marina and the old walls, with a small courtyard. Beaches and boat trips along the cliffs are within walking distance. Features: courtyard, two bathrooms, air conditioning, fast internet, cot on request.
- **The Cape House, Sagres.** A plain, wind-proof house at the south-west tip, for surfers and walkers. The cape and the fishing harbour are close; the sea is often big here. Features: outdoor shower, board and bike store, wood stove, drying room, parking.
