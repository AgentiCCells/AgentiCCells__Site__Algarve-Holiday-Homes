Colophon: a made-up example

Cinco Chaminés is an invented company and this is not a real magazine. The five homes, their descriptions, prices and availability are made up; every picture is an AI-generated picture of an invented house or an imagined Algarve view, not a photograph. The weather, sea, UV and warnings are IPMA's, and the events come from the towns' own agendas, both read live in your browser through data.promptugal.news. Nothing can be booked here.
