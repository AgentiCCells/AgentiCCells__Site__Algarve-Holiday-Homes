The people Invented Synthetic users

    This is a demo of ...

    • Simulated a small holiday-home business run with agents, played like a game: invented visitors arrive, ask the site's agent, book, travel, stay, have good and bad days, and leave reviews, some of them unhappy.
    • Simulated invented admins (an owner, a housekeeper, a bookkeeper) moving rates, cleaning, reordering stock, registering guests and drafting the tourist tax.
    • Simulated Agent Behaviour Policies at several levels in the browser (the site's agent, each assistant, each person), refusing prompt-injection attempts and handing large sums to a person.
    • Simulated a profit and loss for the run, with VAT, commission and costs (not legal or tax advice).
    • Real the weather in the bubbles, as IPMA issued it, and only for the days IPMA forecast; the days of the run stay inside the dates for which real data exists.
    • Invented the company, the five homes, prices, rules and every person. AI-generated every picture of a home.

    The world runs in your browser and writes to the same simulation as the admin: open it in another tab to see this run's bookings. Nothing leaves this browser; Reset clears it. Seeded: the same seed gives the same run.
