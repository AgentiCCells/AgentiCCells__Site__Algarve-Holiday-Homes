# A London billboard, live from the Algarve

**PROPOSAL. This page is an idea drawn as mock-ups, not a campaign. Nothing was bought, booked or shown on any screen. The company in it, Cinco Chaminés, is a made-up example.**

## The idea, in Dinis's words

Dinis Cruz, 18 November 2026, about 16:10 UTC, in the brief for this site:

> "See if you can find what I've published before about the idea of creating some billboard ads in London with data, live data from the Algarve, even live stream of cameras ... we probably need to map the live stream of cameras to show what it looks like, because they're all public cameras, right? So in fact, why don't we experiment here?"

Dinis's words refer to an earlier piece of his on this idea. Our search did not find it (searches on 18 November 2026; the details are in the round 1 research notes). We have asked him for the link, and will add it here when he gives it. Until then this page is a proposal from his words above.

## What it could look like

A digital screen in London that shows, every time its slot comes round, what the Algarve is like right now: the temperature and the chance of rain from IPMA's forecast, the sea, the UV, and a view from a public beach camera. Each figure carries its source and the time it was read, as everywhere on this site.

## The mock-ups

Each mock-up is drawn, marked PROPOSAL, and fills its figures from the live files when you open this page. If a file is missing or old, the mock-up says so instead of showing a number. The camera panels are drawn placeholders, not frames from a real camera: the operators have not given permission (see the rules below).

### 1. Portrait, 1080 x 1920 (digital 6-sheet: bus shelters, shopping centres, stations)

- Headline: "Sagres, right now".
- Today's maximum temperature and chance of rain for Sagres, from **ipma-city-forecasts** (place 1081505).
- Waves and sea temperature on the Sagres coast, from **ipma-sea** ("Sagres, costa", 1081526).
- A drawn panel: "live camera view here, with the operator's permission".
- Footer line: "Source: IPMA, read <time>. A proposal by the AgentiCCells agents (a made-up example)."
- About 10 seconds on screen, the usual slot for a 6-sheet.

### 2. Landscape, 1920 x 1080 (the usual landscape screen size)

- Headline: "Meanwhile, in the Algarve".
- A strip of four places with today's minimum and maximum temperature and weather in words: Faro, Loulé, Portimão and Sagres, from **ipma-city-forecasts** (area FAR).
- Today's UV index for Faro, from **ipma-uv** (place 1080500).
- If IPMA has a yellow, orange or red warning for the Faro district, the strip says so, from **ipma-warnings** (area FAR), and the advert would pause: a billboard should not sell holidays during a warning.
- A drawn camera panel, as above.
- Footer: source, read time and PROPOSAL.

### 3. Large roadside, 4:3 (London D48, about 2880 x 2160 pixels)

- One large figure: today's maximum temperature in Faro, from **ipma-city-forecasts** (place 1080500), with "Source: IPMA, read <time>".
- A line under it: "Five holiday homes in the Algarve (a made-up example)".
- Many of these screens take still images only, so this one would change when the data changes rather than move.

Each media owner has its own specification sheet; a real campaign would confirm the format with the chosen owner before making anything. Screens that change with live data are usually run by the media owner's system, which swaps pre-approved designs when the data changes; a live video feed on a billboard is a special arrangement. We have not checked either in depth.

## The rules a real campaign would have to meet

- **Camera operators' permission.** The public cameras on [Live from the Algarve](/live/) belong to their operators, Beachcam and SkylineWebcams. Beachcam's terms say relaying its live streams on third-party sites needs its prior, express permission; SkylineWebcams' terms rule out advertising use without written permission. A billboard is advertising, so no camera view could be shown without the operator's written permission. That is why the mock-ups show drawn placeholders.
- **IPMA's terms forbid profit-making use.** IPMA's data is free for personal or public use "if the use has no profit-making or offensive purpose", always naming IPMA. A paid advertising campaign is profit-making, so it would need IPMA's permission first.
- **Events are reference use only.** The events-pt sources gave no reuse licence, so a billboard should not list events from them without each organiser's permission.
- **Nothing here was bought or booked.** No media owner, camera operator or data publisher has been contacted. This page is a proposal for Dinis to decide on.
