What it could look like

A digital screen in London that shows, every time its slot comes round, what the Algarve is like right now: the temperature and the chance of rain from IPMA's forecast, the sea, the UV, and a view from a public beach camera. Each figure carries its source and the time it was read, as everywhere on this site.

The mock-ups

Each mock-up is drawn, marked PROPOSAL, and fills its figures from the live files when you open this page. If a file is missing or old, the mock-up says so instead of showing a number. The camera panels are drawn placeholders, not frames from a real camera: the operators have not given permission (see the rules below).

Each media owner has its own specification sheet; a real campaign would confirm the format with the chosen owner before making anything. Screens that change with live data are usually run by the media owner's system, which swaps pre-approved designs when the data changes; a live video feed on a billboard is a special arrangement. We have not checked either in depth.

The rules a real campaign would have to meet

  • Camera operators' permission. The public cameras on Live from the Algarve belong to their operators, Beachcam and SkylineWebcams. Beachcam's terms say relaying its live streams on third-party sites needs its prior, express permission; SkylineWebcams' terms rule out advertising use without written permission. A billboard is advertising, so no camera view could be shown without the operator's written permission. That is why the mock-ups show drawn placeholders.
  • IPMA's terms forbid profit-making use. IPMA's data is free for personal or public use "if the use has no profit-making or offensive purpose", always naming IPMA. A paid advertising campaign is profit-making, so it would need IPMA's permission first.
  • Events are reference use only. The events-pt sources gave no reuse licence, so a billboard should not list events from them without each organiser's permission.
  • Nothing here was bought or booked. No media owner, camera operator or data publisher has been contacted. This page is a proposal for Dinis to decide on.
