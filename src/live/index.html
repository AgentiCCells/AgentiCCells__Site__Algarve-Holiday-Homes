Live from the Algarve

See the Algarve as it is right now: beaches, marinas and towns near the five homes of this made-up example, through public live cameras. Each camera is published by its operator on the operator's own website; we list them and link to them.

How we chose them

  • Public cameras only, published for anyone to watch by the operator that runs them: beach, marina and town views.
  • Never police, traffic-enforcement or private CCTV. We did not consider any.
  • Operators named as their own pages name them. At the time of checking, the cameras here come from two operators: Beachcam (MEO Beachcam) and SkylineWebcams.
  • Link out, do not embed. Both operators' terms need their permission before their live streams are shown on another site: Beachcam's terms ask for prior, express permission to relay its streams, and SkylineWebcams' terms allow embedding only through its own embed code and rule out commercial or advertising use without written permission. So each link opens the camera on the operator's own page, and this site shows no video and no screenshots.
  • Each camera with its address and the date we checked it. Pages change and cameras go offline; if a link does not work, the operator may have moved or stopped that camera.

The cameras

Near each home

The five made-up homes, drawn (the drawings are the site's first pictures of them; the houses are invented).

  • The Salt House, Tavira: we found no camera in Tavira; the nearest are at Praia Verde and Alagoa (Altura), in Castro Marim to the east, and Faro to the west.
  • The Lagoon House, Faro: Praia de Faro and Ilha do Farol (Culatra).
  • The Hill House, near Loulé: Quarteira and Vilamoura, on Loulé's coast.
  • The Harbour House, Lagos: Meia Praia, Praia da Luz and Porto de Mós.
  • The Cape House, Sagres: Sagres, with Praia do Amado and Arrifana further up the west coast.
